# Image prompts (Nano Banana / Gemini image models)

Paste the shared style first, then one slot prompt. Make 3 to 4 variants per slot, keep the best 2 to 4.

Save candidates as `images/candidates/<slot>-1.jpg`, `-2.jpg`, `-3.jpg`, `-4.jpg` (these show on `pick-images.html`).
After Carly picks, copy the winner to `images/<slot>.jpg`. The book swaps the placeholder box for it automatically.

Check every image for wrong small details (goose wings, rosary beads, candle holders, any stray text).
The author photo should be a real photo, not generated.

## Shared style

```
Photorealistic fine-art photograph, shot on a full-frame camera with an 85mm lens at f/2.8, natural light, soft film-like color, gentle grain, quiet devotional mood. 3:2 landscape. Keep the main subject in the center 70% of the frame so it survives a tighter crop on a phone. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no people's faces.
```

## `leaves`: Seasons & Nature (Colors of Fall)

```
Pressed autumn leaves in deep red, amber and gold, arranged loosely on a weathered cream linen cloth on an old wooden table, lit by low golden-hour window light from the left, shallow depth of field, tiny dust motes in the light, peaceful and warm.
```

## `geese`: Seasons & Nature (Observing Autumn)

```
A small flock of Canada geese flying in a high loose V formation across a pale, hazy autumn sky, soft peach and grey clouds, bare tree branches at the very bottom edge of the frame, lots of calm empty sky, quiet sense of wonder.
```

## `light`: Faith & Resilience (If and When, Walk On)

```
Rays of golden light breaking through dark storm clouds over a distant open field, the clouds parting in the center of the frame, wet grass glowing below, dramatic but gentle, hopeful, no buildings or people.
```

## `candles`: Time & Reflection (The Faceless Clock)

```
Several small votive candles burning in clear glass holders on a stone ledge inside a quiet old chapel, warm flames, soft bokeh of a dim arched window behind them, long exposure feel, still and contemplative.
```

## `window`: Love & Family (Missing My Son)

```
Soft morning light through a lace-curtained window falling onto a wooden windowsill, a small wooden rosary resting beside a framed empty photo frame turned slightly away from the camera, a single dried flower in a tiny glass, tender and nostalgic.
```

## `lace`: Life's Complexities (Ashes of the Vow)

```
Dried pressed roses and baby's breath resting on aged, slightly yellowed vintage lace, muted dusty colors, soft overcast window light, close overhead angle with shallow focus, quietly melancholy, the passage of time.
```
